06 · Interview Prep

Talking points
that land

Q1
Walk me through your Terraform state management
Remote state in S3 with DynamoDB locking — prevents concurrent apply conflicts in team settings. S3 versioning lets you roll back to any previous state. The bootstrap script provisions both before first init.
Q2
How does your VPC design handle security?
Three-tier isolation — ALB in public subnets, EC2 in private (no direct internet), RDS in DB subnets with no route to IGW. Security groups enforce least privilege per tier.
Q3
What's your rollback strategy?
Terraform state history + terraform state rollback. For the app layer, Ansible idempotency means re-running always converges to desired state. ASG handles instance-level failures automatically.
Q4
Tell me about your FinOps feature
Lambda polls Cost Explorer after every apply, calculates per-service cost, and posts a structured breakdown to Slack. Dashboard on S3 shows a 7-day cost trend. No engineer should deploy blind to cost.
